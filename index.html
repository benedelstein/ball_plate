Getting
here

✦
01 Fly into the Bay +

San Francisco International Airport (SFO) is the simplest option and about 25 minutes from central San Francisco without traffic. Oakland International Airport (OAK) is also convenient from the East Bay.

Visit SFO ↗
02 Where to stay +

Stay in San Francisco and make a weekend of it. Hayes Valley, the Mission, Pacific Heights, Nob Hill, and Union Square all put the city within easy reach. Book early: August is a popular time to visit.

03 Getting around +

Rideshare is plentiful, BART connects both airports to the city, and Muni is great for exploring. Bring a light layer—the famous San Francisco summer can turn cool after sunset.
